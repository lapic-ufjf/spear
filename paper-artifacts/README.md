# Think-Aloud Study

Materials from the Think-Aloud sessions used to evaluate **SPEAR**
(System for Planning, Executing, and Analyzing Research on Web).

## Study

Ten participants were asked to design, in advance, a human-subject
experiment of their own choice, on any topic, provided it involved tasks
performed through a Web-based system.

Before the session, each participant received
[`web-experiments-primer.pdf`](web-experiments-primer.pdf), covering research
questions, experimental conditions, between- and within-subjects designs,
experiments versus quasi-experiments, the typical participant flow, and
ethical practices. The document also stated the preparation task.

During the session, each participant used SPEAR to model their own proposed
experiment while continuously verbalizing their reasoning. Sessions lasted
approximately 35 minutes on average. No post-session questionnaire was
administered; the analysis relies exclusively on the verbalizations.

## Contents

```
think-aloud-study/
├── README.md
├── web-experiments-primer.pdf    # material sent to participants beforehand
└── transcripts/
    ├── P01.txt
    └── ... P10.txt
```

Transcripts are plain UTF-8 text, one paragraph per turn, prefixed by the
speaker label:

```
Pesquisador: <turn>

P06: <turn>
```

## Anonymization

- Participants are identified as **P01–P10**. The session conductor appears
  as **Pesquisador**; where a second person was present, as **Pesquisador 2**.
- Timestamps were removed. No emails, affiliations, or other personal
  identifiers remain.
- Audio and video recordings are not published, as they cannot be anonymized.

Transcripts are otherwise verbatim: hesitations, repetitions, and false
starts were preserved, since they carry meaning in a Think-Aloud protocol.

## Language

Sessions were conducted in **Portuguese**, and both the transcripts and the
primer are kept in the original language.
